## Sources and timing differences

- The 1997 text was checked through public Open Library search-inside passages; the whole book was not read and page numbers were not available. [Catalogue record](https://archive.org/details/fourthturningame0000stra), [publisher excerpt](https://penguinrandomhousehighereducation.com/book/?isbn=9780767900461).
- The 2008 start event: [Howe's own MacroVoices interview, 4 April 2019](https://www.macrovoices.com/podcast-transcripts/544-neil-howe-super-bullish-the-u-s-a-in-the-2030s-but-between-now-and-then). This is a later choice, not an event named in 1997. NBER's 2007 peak year is a different measure.
- The later deadline for the great passage: [an excerpt from Howe's 2023 book, republished 23 August 2024](https://members.porterandcompanyresearch.com/our-rendezvous-with-destiny/). A named book excerpt published by a third party, not the site editor's own forecast. No claim about Howe's latest position in 2026.
- Climax and resolution are kept apart: [Simon & Schuster's description of the 2023 book](https://www.simonandschuster.com/books/The-Fourth-Turning-Is-Here/Neil-Howe/9781982173739) describes the climax. [Howe's own interview, 23 August 2023, at 58:41](https://www.toptradersunplugged.com/podcast/how-to-survive-the-current-fourth-turning-ft-neil-howe/) gives a resolution estimate in the early 2030s. They are not synonyms.
